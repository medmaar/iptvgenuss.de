Multilingual website for learning German

deutsch.info is a multilingual online platform for learning German - everywhere, at any time and for free! Besides online courses, grammar explanations, games and audio-visual materials, you'll find practical information about working and living in Germany and Austria as well as free teaching materials.

Register now – for free!

What can you find here?

Courses

Four comprehensive language courses at levels A1, A2, B1 and B2, with interactive exercises and audio-visual teaching materials

Grammar

The most important rules, with example sentences, for the correct usage of German grammar

Media library

Multimedia content: numerous authentic texts as well as audio and visual material in German

Background

Information about life in Germany and Austria – practical and especially important for migrants

Quiz

Playful knowledge tests about history, culture, politics and society in Germany and Austria with authentic questions from citizenship tests.

Teaching materials

Free and creative teaching materials for German as foreign language and German as a second language.

Did you know?

The Austrian word 'Busserl' means 'Küsschen' – a kiss, peck on the cheek .
